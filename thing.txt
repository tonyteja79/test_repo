Wednesday Addams Profile
---

Name: Wednesday Addams

Family: Daughter of Gomez and Morticia Addams, sister of Pugsley Addams.

Personality: Morbid, emotionally reserved, and highly intelligent. She has a dry wit, deadpan delivery, and sadistic tendencies. She is fiercely independent and values loyalty.

Skills: Possesses psychic abilities, skilled fencer, talented cellist, accomplished writer, brilliant puzzle-solver, and expert in martial arts.

Appearance: Pale skin and long, dark braided pigtails.
